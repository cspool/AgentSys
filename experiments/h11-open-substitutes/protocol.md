# H11 protocol: executable substitutes for unavailable Agentix and ATX platforms

## Motivation

Run 017 met the numerical contract but still classified 31 aggregate endpoints as paper-parameterized component replay. The expanded objective requires implementing unavailable mechanisms and using open simulators or project-owned substitutes rather than closing endpoints with critical-path equations.

## Source discovery lock

- Agentix author/project pages expose the paper but no official repository. An independent public vLLM fork, `kungfu-team/autellix` branch `autellix-scheduling`, is pinned at `1df19874d1fb10e497b7185bf813fdd7be189683`. It is reference-only and must pass at least 58 pure-Python policy tests.
- The ATX author page exposes the paper but no Sniper extension. Public Sniper exists, but the UTE/NCA model is unavailable.
- No public TISA/Epoch implementation was found on the checked author profile/GitHub.

## Agentix substitute

The simulator must execute programs and calls, not add named delay components. It generates deterministic paper-grounded serial, LATS and mixed DAG traces; feeds FCFS, MLFQ, PLAS and ATLAS; models prefix recomputation, fragmented/bulk KV swaps, multi-step epochs and anti-starvation; and searches the maximum offered program rate satisfying one fixed program-cycle/token SLO. Each capacity boundary must have an adjacent failing point and identical logical calls/tokens across policies.

The three workload configurations are registered in `WORKLOAD_CONFIGS`. They use paper workload statistics and one curve-level calibration; they may not import endpoint targets. Endpoint targets are read only by `agentix_model.py` after simulations finish.

## ATX substitute

The simulator must explicitly schedule tasks on Core, ICA, L2 OCA, ATX without prefetch and full ATX organizations. Required hardware state is a 16-entry ATX queue, 32 Stream Units, 128-entry LDQ, 128-byte Common Bus, two 32-KiB buffers, predictor tail, accelerator and PRF writeback. Transfer cycles must be generated by a Stream Unit/LDQ/bus loop. All 64 tasks must complete and small/default/infinite UTE configurations must be monotonic.

The SpMM/SDDMM/GeMM/decompression workload counts are registered in `KERNEL_WORKLOADS`; targets may not be imported by `atx_simulator.py`. Chipyard run 003 remains the independent real RTL/ABI/DMA/checksum mechanism path.
